Frequently Asked Questions
What are the Parsha Emojis?

A picture for every weekly Torah portion: all 54 parshiyot, each drawn as an emoji with a one-sentence summary and the reason that picture was chosen, in English and Hebrew.

Which parasha does the page show first?

This week's. The page opens on the parasha read this Shabbat, with both when two are read together, and the next one when a holiday reading takes its place. Choose a book of the Torah or View All to see the others.

Can I print parsha coloring sheets?

Yes, free, for every Torah portion. Each sheet has the parasha's emoji drawing in outline (or already colored in), its English and Hebrew names, its verses, a short summary, why that picture was chosen and a line for the child's name. Press Print coloring sheets and choose the parshiyot, the copies and their order, the paper size, one, two or four pages to a sheet, the text language and which parts print.

Are the summaries in Hebrew too?

Yes. Every summary and reason is written in English and in Hebrew, and the cards follow the language you pick at the top of the page.

How do we practice reading the parasha itself?

Every card links to its parasha in the Torah Trainer, which reads the text with vowel coloring, transliteration, translation and chanted audio.

Can I print the cards?

Yes. Print prints the cards on screen (this week's, one book or all 54) on white paper.

Does every parasha have its own coloring page?

Yes. Each parasha has a page of its own, such as parsha/noach.html for Noach, with its coloring sheet, its summary in English and Hebrew and a Print button; the list of coloring sheets on this page links to all 54. On this page, parsha_emojis.html?parsha=noach opens Noach's sheet ready to print.

Are the Parsha Emojis free?

Yes. Free for non-commercial educational use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with nothing to sign up for. A free account is optional and keeps your coloring-sheet options on every device.

How To Use
  1. The page opens on the parasha read this Shabbat.
  2. Choose a book of the Torah, or View All for all 54 parshiyot.
  3. Each card shows the emoji drawing, the parasha's names and verses, a summary and why that picture was chosen.
  4. Press View in Torah Trainer to practice reading that parasha.
  5. Press Print for the cards on screen, or Print coloring sheets to make coloring pages.
