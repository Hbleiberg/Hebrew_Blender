About & FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Torah Trainer?

A free Hebrew Torah reader. Pick a parsha and read it with optional transliteration, English translation, per-vowel color coding, cantillation strip, prose TTS, and chanted-audio karaoke that highlights words in time with the recording. When you are done reading, copy the passage out as formatted text or print it as a classroom sheet.

Does it include chanted-audio karaoke?

Yes. PocketTorah's recordings of all 54 parshiyot and haftarot stream on demand, with word-level highlighting synchronized to the audio. Adjust playback speed without re-fetching.

Where do the Hebrew texts and translations come from?

Sefaria's public API. The translation dropdown defaults to JPS 1985 when available; pick any other version from the same dropdown.

Can I hide nikkud or cantillation marks?

Yes. The Display panel has separate toggles for nikkud (vowel points) and cantillation marks (te'amim). They can be hidden independently.

Can it color-code the Torah trope (cantillation) families?

Yes. Trope color coding tints each word by its cantillation clause family — Sof Pasuk, Zakef Katon, Segol, Revia, Geresh, and rare marks — with a legend above the reading. It composes with vowel color coding, keeps working when the cantillation marks themselves are hidden, and each family's color is customizable in settings.

Diaspora or Israel schedule?

The default is diaspora. Flip the Reading Schedule toggle to switch to Israel. Use "Jump to this week's parsha" to auto-pick the current week's reading from Sefaria's calendar.

Can I copy the Hebrew text out of the reader?

Yes. The Copy panel in the Options drawer copies the passage as rich text, keeping the vowel and trope colors, so it pastes into Google Docs, Word, or Pages. Copy one verse, select several, or type a verse range, and choose what travels with it — nikkud, cantillation marks, transliteration, translation, verse numbers, or blank lines for handwriting.

Is it free?

Yes. Materials incorporating PocketTorah audio are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; other IvritSuite content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

How To Use
  1. Pick a parsha from the top dropdown (defaults to this week's). The Reading dropdown shows each aliyah's verse range, and 🎵 marks readings with chanted audio.
  2. Toggle Translit and Translation; choose Side-by-side or Interlinear layout.
  3. Customize the Hebrew font, vowel coloring, and translit scheme in this drawer.
  4. Enable Karaoke and pick an aliyah from the Reading dropdown to chant along. Use 🔁 Loop on any verse to repeat it hands-free for practice.
  5. On a full parsha, use the aliyah jump chips at the top to skip around, and the Resume chip to pick up where you left off.
  6. Click the fullscreen button to project to a SmartBoard.
  7. New here? Press ❓ Tour in the header for a quick walkthrough.
