About & FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Torah Trainer?

A free Hebrew Torah reader. Pick a parsha and read it with optional transliteration, English translation, per-vowel color coding, cantillation strip, prose TTS, and chanted-audio karaoke that highlights words in time with the recording.

Does it include chanted-audio karaoke?

Yes. PocketTorah's recordings of all 54 parshiyot and haftarot stream on demand, with word-level highlighting synchronized to the audio. Adjust playback speed without re-fetching.

Where do the Hebrew texts and translations come from?

Sefaria's public API. The translation dropdown defaults to JPS 1985 when available; pick any other version from the same dropdown.

Can I hide nikkud or cantillation marks?

Yes. The Display panel has separate toggles for nikkud (vowel points) and cantillation marks (te'amim). They can be hidden independently.

Diaspora or Israel schedule?

The default is diaspora. Flip the Reading Schedule toggle to switch to Israel. Use "Jump to this week's parsha" to auto-pick the current week's reading from Sefaria's calendar.

Is it free?

Yes. Materials incorporating PocketTorah audio are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; other IvritSuite content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

How To Use
  1. Pick a parsha from the top dropdown (defaults to this week's).
  2. Toggle Translit and Translation; choose Side-by-side or Interlinear layout.
  3. Customize the Hebrew font, vowel coloring, and translit scheme in this drawer.
  4. Enable Karaoke and pick an aliyah from the Reading dropdown to chant along.
  5. Click the fullscreen button to project to a SmartBoard.
