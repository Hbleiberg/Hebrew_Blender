          Frequently Asked Questions
          How do flash cards help reading?

          Hebrew flash cards build automatic letter–vowel recognition, the core skill behind fluent reading. Short drills with nikkud help students decode unfamiliar words faster.

          Are they free?

          Yes. Free for non-commercial educational use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

          Do cards include nikkud?

          Yes. Every card displays Hebrew letters with full nikkud, and you can choose which vowels appear.

          Can I customize letters?

          Yes. Pick any subset of Hebrew letters and vowels, and 1, 2, or 3 letters per card.

          How To Use
          1. Choose 1, 2, or 3 letters per card.
          2. Select Hebrew letters and vowels to drill.
          3. Set the number of cards in the deck.
          4. Click Start to begin.
          5. Tap a card to reveal it; mark right or wrong.
