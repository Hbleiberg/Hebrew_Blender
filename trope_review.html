Trope traditions review

Each of the twenty traditions of docs/trope-traditions.md was transcribed from scans with AI help. Check every row against its source below; when a tradition is right, press Approve and make the one-line change it names in data/trope/trope_approvals.json (in a commit, as any edit). The pages then offer that tradition to everyone. Until then they show it only on a ?preview=traditions visit, marked Unverified transcription.

How to read a row. The staff is drawn exactly as the pages draw it, in the key the tradition is written in. Under each row, the things most worth checking against the scan:
  • chromatic notes — each one's spelling as the staff draws it (the six JE columns whose minor-sixth step is spelled as a raised fifth draw G♯ in C, D♯ in G);
  • a printed 3 kept as written (3!{) — a bracket numbered 3 whose notes do not add up to a triplet; the 127 that do were made true triplets (two-thirds length) on import;
  • pickups (Spiro's notes in parentheses) and fermatas.
The import also let each syllable run to the next one (covering the rests after it), made a JE sign's name span its sign, and started the telisha syllable of the Common Torah melody's rows 9 and 10 on its first note. To correct a note, edit its row in docs/trope-traditions.md (the line is named beside each row), run node scripts/build-trope-phrases.mjs, bump trope_phrases.json's ?v= on both pages and sw.js's VERSION — the fingerprint then changes, and the tradition needs approving again.
