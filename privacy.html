Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

1. Introduction

IvritSuite (the "site") is a free collection of Hebrew literacy tools for teachers and students, operated by Harrison Bleiberg. This Privacy Policy explains what information the site collects, what it stores only when you ask it to, how that information is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

IvritSuite runs in your browser. You never need an account, and unless you sign in and choose to upload something, the site stores no personal data on a server. Specifically:

  • No personal information without an account. Using the tools does not ask for, transmit, or store your name, email address, school, class list, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Tool content stays in your browser. Anything you create with the tools — worksheet presets, classroom-dashboard schedules and settings, flash-card decks and preferences, word lists, dictionary filters, Torah Trainer and Trope Tutor settings — is stored locally in your browser using the standard localStorage API. It never leaves your device through any channel operated by the site unless you upload it (next point).
  • An optional account, for adults. If you sign in (with an emailed code or a Google account), the site stores your email address (and, for Google, the name Google shares), a display name, and only the items you choose to upload from a tool's Cloud saves panel or the Font Maker's Save to my account: presets, decks, settings, word lists, mastery progress, your suite-wide preferences (language, theme, keyboard layout, default Hebrew font and size, the name you sign fonts with, and dictionary display choices), the fonts you added to My Fonts, Font Maker projects (with the letter photos they were traced from and the fonts exported from them), and — when a teacher decides to upload them — student names and practice results from Flash Cards, or class lists from the Classroom Dashboard. Nothing is uploaded automatically. Everything in an account is visible only to that account, is never sold or shared, and can be downloaded as a file or deleted by you at any time (Section 5).
  • No tracking pixels or fingerprinting. For aggregate usage statistics the site uses only Google Analytics 4, described in Section 3.

3. Cookies & Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-CMLC81Z0HC) to understand which tools are used and how visitors arrive at the site. GA4 collects aggregated, anonymized data such as page views, approximate geographic region, device class, and referral source. IP addresses are anonymized by GA4 by default and are not stored by the site operator.

You can control or block analytics in several ways:

  • Use your browser's "Do Not Track" or tracking-protection settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Block third-party scripts via your preferred ad/tracker blocker.

For details on Google's data handling, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Resources

Some tools fetch or link to third-party content. Visiting or interacting with these resources is governed by their own privacy policies, not by IvritSuite:

  • Kaikki.org — source of the Hebrew word data used in the dictionary and worksheet generator.
  • PocketTorah — chanted-audio source used by the Torah Trainer.
  • Google Fonts — Hebrew and Latin web fonts.
  • Public weather APIs used by the Classroom Dashboard to display current local weather (only the location you enter is sent, in the standard form required to look up a forecast).
  • Supabase — the sign-in service and database behind optional accounts (Section 2). It processes your sign-in email and stores the items you upload, on servers in the United States (Amazon Web Services, Oregon). Each account can reach only its own data, and the site operator never uses that data for anything but giving it back to you. Supabase's own privacy policy applies to its handling of the service.
  • Web3Forms and hCaptcha — used only if you send a message through the contact page or the "Suggest a Resource" form on the resources page. The information you enter in those forms is passed to Web3Forms solely to deliver the email to the site operator, and the form uses hCaptcha to block automated spam; both process that data under their own privacy policies. Nothing from the form is stored by IvritSuite.

5. Local Storage & Your Control Over Data

Your settings live in your browser, and anything in an account is a copy you control:

  • The Import / Export All Settings button on the home page lets you download a single JSON backup of every tool's data, restore from a backup, or erase everything in one click.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for IvritSuite removes all stored settings and presets. Signing out, or Erase All Settings, also removes the account session from that device.
  • Using a different browser, device, or private/incognito window gives you a fresh, separate set of settings — unless you sign in there and download your cloud copies.
  • In every tool's Cloud saves panel you can see exactly what your account holds, download any item as a file, or delete it from the cloud; the copy on your device is never deleted by the site. Font Maker projects are listed under Load Project ▾ → In your account, where each can be opened or deleted.
  • Your account page shows everything your account holds, downloads all of it as one file, and deletes the account with everything in it — right away, with no email to write.

6. Children's Privacy

IvritSuite is designed to be safely usable by students of all ages, including children under 13, and the tools never require an account. Accounts are meant for adults — teachers, parents and tutors — and a student never has to enter an email address to use anything on the site. The site does not knowingly collect personal information from children; if an account turns out to belong to a child under 13, we delete it as soon as we learn of it. If you believe any personal information has been collected in violation of this policy, please contact us through the contact page and we will remove it.

7. FERPA Compliance For Schools

This section is provided for school administrators, IT departments, and educators evaluating IvritSuite for classroom use under the U.S. Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 CFR Part 99).

No collection of student records; storage only when a teacher chooses it

IvritSuite does not collect, receive, or use personally identifiable information (PII) from student education records on its own. The site has no grade books and no submission of student work, and by default everything a teacher or student creates — worksheets, presets, decks, schedules, tool preferences, practice results — is stored exclusively in the local browser using localStorage and remains on the user's own device. A teacher who signs in may choose to upload items from a tool to their own account; today that can include a Flash Cards student profile (a student's name and practice results) or a Classroom Dashboard class list (student names). Such uploads are stored for that teacher only, are visible only to that account, are never used, sold, or shared by the site operator, and can be deleted by the teacher at any time. Whether a teacher may store student names in an online service is governed by the school's own policies; teachers who prefer not to simply never upload student profiles or class lists, and everything stays on the device.

Analytics do not constitute education records

The site's only data collection is anonymized, aggregated Google Analytics 4 usage data (page views, device class, region). GA4 anonymizes IP addresses and is not associated with any student identifier; this data is not directly or indirectly linkable to individual students and therefore does not constitute "education records" or "personally identifiable information" under 34 CFR § 99.3.

School-official designation is not required

Using the tools discloses no PII or education records to the site, so schools and districts may permit teachers and students to use IvritSuite without entering into a separate data-sharing or "school official" agreement under the FERPA exception at 34 CFR § 99.31(a)(1). Uploading student profiles or class lists to a teacher's account is optional and off by default; a district that wants to allow it and requires a written acknowledgment of these practices may rely on this policy as a public statement of them, or ask us for one through the contact page.

No advertising or sale of data

IvritSuite does not sell, rent, license, or otherwise share any user information with advertisers or data brokers. The site displays no advertising.

Parental review and access

The site holds no student records of its own, so there is nothing for parents to inspect, amend, or have removed on the site's side unless a teacher has uploaded a student profile or a class list to their account. In that case the teacher can show, correct or delete it from the tool's Cloud saves panel (Download file / Delete from cloud), and parents may also ask us through the contact page. Locally stored tool settings can be reviewed directly in the browser used by the student via the home-page Import / Export All Settings control.

FERPA-related questions

For FERPA-specific inquiries, please contact Harrison Bleiberg through the contact page.

8. Security

The site is delivered over HTTPS. Without an account, no user data is transmitted to a server operated by the site, so the primary security boundary is your own browser and device. Data in an account is stored by Supabase, encrypted in transit and at rest, and each account can read only its own rows and files (enforced in the database, not just in the page). The site keeps no passwords — sign-in uses one-time emailed codes or Google. The site does not promise backups of cloud data: keep your own .ivrit files. We recommend keeping your browser up to date and using device-level protections appropriate to your setting.
