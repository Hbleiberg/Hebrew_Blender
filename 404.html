דַּף לֹא נִמְצָא

This page wandered off for forty years.

We searched right-to-left and left-to-right, but this page is nowhere to be found (לֹא נִמְצָא, lo nimtza). It may have slipped out for a snack and forgotten its vowels.

We tried to blend it back together from a few stray letters — but without the nikkud, nobody could agree on how to pronounce it.
