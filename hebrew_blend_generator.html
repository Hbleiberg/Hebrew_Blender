Frequently Asked Questions
What is a Hebrew blend worksheet?

A Hebrew blend worksheet pairs Hebrew letters with vowels (nikkud) so students can practice decoding syllables one, two, or three letters at a time.

Are these Hebrew worksheets free?

Yes. All worksheets are free under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for non-commercial educational use.

Can I print the worksheets?

Yes. The generator outputs print-ready PDFs sized for standard letter and A4 paper.

Do they include nikkud?

Yes. Choose any combination of nikkud — kamatz, patach, segol, tzere, hirik, holam, kubutz, shuruk, shva, and hataf vowels.

What ages is it for?

Designed for emerging Hebrew readers — elementary students and adult beginners — but fully customizable for any level.

How To Use
  1. Pick a practice type (1, 2, or 3 letters).
  2. Select the Hebrew letters to include.
  3. Select the nikkud (vowels) to include.
  4. Click Generate Worksheet.
  5. Click Print or Export PDF to download.
