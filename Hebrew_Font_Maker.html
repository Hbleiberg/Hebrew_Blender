English letters
Adds an English tab to design A–Z and a–z (with an uppercase/lowercase switch). Accent-supporting letters can carry accents (á, é, ñ, ç…). Template: PDF · PNG
Specialized letters
Adds a Specialized letters tab with the wide letterforms (wide alef, dalet, he, kaf, lamed, final mem, resh, tav) — auto-composed from your traced letters, each with a Width slider that adds space & stretches.
Yiddish
Adds a Yiddish tab: pasekh/komets alef (אַ אָ), the rafe letters (בֿ כֿ פֿ), and the digraphs tsvey vovn / vov-yud / tsvey yudn (װ ױ ײ) — all composed from your traced letters.
Ladino
Adds a Ladino (Judeo-Spanish) tab: the rafe / varika letters (בֿ גֿ דֿ זֿ טֿ פֿ קֿ) plus שׁ / שׂ — composed from your traced letters.
Custom glyphs
Adds a Custom glyphs tab (after Punctuation) where you can define glyphs at any Unicode code point — a main letter or a nikkud mark. Tip: for brand-new shapes use the Private-Use range U+E000–U+F8FF so they don't collide with standard characters.

Hebrew Font Maker — a free, experimental IvritSuite tool. Builds real fonts entirely client-side (the font engine loads on first export), with GPOS mark positioning for nikkud (mark-to-base) and cantillation/trop (mark-to-mark). Exports TrueType (TTF), WOFF2 and editable UFO source, and your finished font can be used in other IvritSuite tools such as Torah Trainer. Created by Harrison Bleiberg. Desktop browser recommended.

Edit mark shape

✍️ Welcome to the Hebrew Font Maker

Turn your own handwriting into a real, installable Hebrew font — trace each letter, place the nikkud & trop, then export TTF/WOFF2 (or save it straight into your other IvritSuite tools). New here? The guide walks through every step.

Apply this alignment to other letters

Copies the current letter's position & scale (its image transform — not its traced outline) onto the letters you pick, overwriting their current alignment. You can undo it.

Apply this positioning to other letters

Copies where this letter's nikkud & trop marks sit (the anchor positions) onto the letters you pick, overwriting theirs. You can undo it.

On: copies the vertical positions and keeps each mark centered on the target letter's own width (best when letters differ in width). Off: copies the exact X positions too.
