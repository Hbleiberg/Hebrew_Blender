Your account

Everything your account holds, a download of all of it, and the way to delete it. What an account stores and how it is protected: Privacy Policy.

Checking your sign-in…

Who

Shown in the header when you are signed in. Only you see it.

What your account holds

Copies of the items you uploaded from the tools. To sync, download or delete a single item, use that tool's Cloud saves panel (Font Maker projects: Load Project ▾ → In your account).

    Download everything

    One .zip file with everything in your account: an .ivrit file of every saved item (restore it with Import / Export All Settings on the home page) and each Font Maker project as a .hebrewfont file with its photos, plus its exported font when you kept one. Photo projects make this large; the size is shown above.

    Delete my account

    This deletes your account and everything in it — every saved item, every Font Maker project with its photos and exported fonts — right away and for good. Nothing on this device or any other device is touched: your browsers keep their own copies, and your .ivrit and .hebrewfont files stay yours.

    Are you sure?

    Your account was deleted

    Your account and everything in it are gone. Everything saved on this device stays exactly as it was, and you can keep using every tool without an account.
