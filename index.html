Free Hebrew dictionary to browse and filter Hebrew vocabulary by theme, length, part of speech, era, and letter position — useful for Hebrew learning at every level.

Hebrew Classroom Dashboard

Live Hebrew date, time, weather, and days of the week for the Hebrew classroom — SmartBoard and projector ready, free for teachers.

Hebrew Flash Cards Beta

Free Hebrew flash cards for reading practice — decode one, two, or three Hebrew letters at a time with full nikkud support. Optimized for mobile.

Practice Hebrew flash cards →

Torah Trainer

Read the weekly parsha with vowel coloring, transliteration, English translations, cantillation toggle, and chanted-audio karaoke (PocketTorah).

Practice Torah reading →

Trope Tutor

Learn the Torah cantillation marks (trope / te'amim) — symbols, Ashkenazi and Sephardi names, and melodies with real chanted examples — then drill yourself with three quiz modes.

Practice trope →

Hebrew Font Maker

Turn your own handwritten Hebrew letters into a real, installable font — trace each letter, place nikkud & trop, then export TTF, WOFF2, or UFO.
