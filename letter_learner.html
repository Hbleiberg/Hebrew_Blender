        Contact & Support
        Frequently Asked Questions
        What is the Letter Learner?

        A free alef-bet tool for beginning readers. Every Hebrew letter is a card that opens a picture to remember it by, four illustrated words, the letter with each vowel read aloud, and the letter in different fonts.

        Who is it for?

        Kindergarten, first-grade and Mechina classes, and anyone meeting the Hebrew letters for the first time. It works on a SmartBoard or projector, a computer, a tablet or a phone.

        Does it teach Hebrew cursive?

        Yes. Letters (Cursive) shows every letter in handwriting beside its printed form, with a picture to remember the shape, the letters it is easy to mix up with, and the same words written by hand.

        How are the vowels taught?

        In Vowels, every vowel has its own card, and each vowel family has three cues that point to the same sound: a name tied to the vowel's shape, a gesture to make while saying it, and a color. Each card also reads the vowel aloud on a few letters.

        How does it read the letters aloud?

        With your device's own Hebrew voice (its built-in text-to-speech). The Options card chooses the voice and whether each word gets a speaker. A device with no Hebrew voice can add one in its speech settings.

        Can I print coloring sheets?

        Yes. Every letter card has a coloring sheet, and Print coloring sheets prints many letters at once: choose the letters, the copies and their order, block or cursive, Letter or A4 paper, one, two or four pages to a sheet, and outlines or already colored in.

        Can I link straight to one letter?

        Yes. Add ?letter= and the letter's name to the address: letter_learner.html?letter=gimel opens the Gimel card, and ?mode=cursive&letter=gimel opens it in cursive. The mode you switch to stays in the address, so a bookmark keeps it.

        Is the Letter Learner free?

        Yes. Free for non-commercial educational use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, with nothing to sign up for. A free account is optional and keeps your options on every device.

        How To Use
        1. Choose Letters (Block), Letters (Cursive) or Vowels at the top of the page.
        2. Tap any letter to open its card, then move between its tabs: the letter with its picture and words, its vowels, its fonts and its coloring sheet.
        3. Press a speaker to hear the letter with a vowel, or a word, read aloud.
        4. Open Options to choose which vowels show, whether pairs like Bet and Vet get their own cards, and whether the words are read aloud.
        5. Print one letter's coloring sheet from its card, or press Print coloring sheets to print many letters at once.
