Frequently Asked Questions
How do I look up a Hebrew word?

Type the Hebrew word (with or without nikkud), or use filters to browse by length, part of speech, era, or letter position.

Can I filter by part of speech?

Yes. Filter by noun, verb, adjective, adverb, and others, combined with length and era filters.

Is it free?

Yes. Free under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; word data sourced from Kaikki.org.

Modern and biblical Hebrew?

Yes. Filter by era — biblical, mishnaic, medieval, and modern Hebrew.

Can I share or save my filters?

Yes. Click 🔗 Copy link in the results toolbar to copy a link that reopens this exact filtered view — send it to your class. Your last-used filters also come back automatically on your next visit. New here? Click ❓ Tour in the header for a quick 7-step walkthrough.

How To Use
  1. Type a Hebrew word in the search bar (with or without nikkud).
  2. Or filter by word length, part of speech, era, or letter position.
  3. Click any result to view the word's details.
