Frequently Asked Questions
How do I look up a Hebrew word?

Type the Hebrew word (with or without nikkud) into the search bar, or use the filters to browse by word length, part of speech, era, or letter position.

Can I filter the Hebrew dictionary by part of speech?

Yes. Filter by noun, verb, adjective, adverb, and other parts of speech, alone or in combination with length and era filters.

Is the Hebrew dictionary free?

Yes. The Hebrew word lookup is free under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; word data is sourced from Kaikki.org.

Does the dictionary include modern and biblical Hebrew?

Yes. Words can be filtered by era, including biblical, mishnaic, medieval, and modern Hebrew.
