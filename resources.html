Frequently Asked Questions
What's in the directory?

A curated set of websites, printables, lesson-planning tools, Tanakh references, Hebrew language sites, and Jewish culture resources.

Are the resources free?

Most are free; some link to paid services. Each card notes when registration or payment is required.

Who is this for?

Hebrew and Jewish-studies teachers, day-school faculty, supplementary-school teachers, parents, and adult learners.

How To Use
  1. Pick a category (Lesson Planning, Printables, Tanakh, Hebrew, Culture, Misc).
  2. Pick an age group (K–2, 3–5, 6–12, Adult/Teacher).
  3. Click any resource card to open it in a new tab.
