Our privacy promise

All numbers on this page are anonymous event counts — IvritSuite never tracks people. When an action is counted, the only things sent are the name of the action, the date, and a 2-letter country code from our content-delivery network. No IP addresses, no names, no accounts, no cookies, and nothing you create. See the Privacy Policy for the full details, including how to opt out.
