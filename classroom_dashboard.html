About & FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Hebrew classroom dashboard for?

It displays the live Hebrew date, time, weather, and days of the week for use on classroom SmartBoards or projectors during morning routine and Hebrew instruction.

Does it work on SmartBoards?

Yes. The dashboard is designed for SmartBoards, projectors, and large displays. A built-in fullscreen mode and zoom control let you size content for any classroom screen.

Is the dashboard free?

Yes. The dashboard is free to use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for non-commercial educational purposes.

Can I project the Hebrew date?

Yes. The Hebrew (Jewish) date and the corresponding Gregorian date are shown live and update automatically each day.

Is there a guided tour, and how do I switch layouts?

Click ❓ Tour in the top bar for a quick walkthrough. First-time teachers also get a setup card. To switch layouts, open Settings → Presets and tap a Quick-start layout — Hebrew classroom, Morning routine, or a General classroom layout for non-Judaics rooms — then tweak it and Save your own preset.

Can the dashboard switch between periods by itself?

Yes. Settings → Schedule Sync lets you assign a preset and an end time to each period; the board reconfigures itself through the day and shows a live Now / Next display and a countdown.

How do I keep the display from going to sleep?

Fullscreen keeps the screen awake automatically. Otherwise turn on Keep screen awake in Settings → Display Options, where the browser supports it.

How To Use
  1. Set your location for accurate weather and Hebrew date.
  2. Pick which panels to show in Display Options.
  3. Customize the Hebrew font and nikkud display.
  4. Use the fullscreen button to project to your SmartBoard.
  5. Save your setup as a preset — or start from a Quick-start layout in the Presets panel.
  6. In fullscreen, move the mouse to reveal quick controls (settings, timer, text, video).
  7. Click the ✎ on the dashboard message to edit it right on the board.
